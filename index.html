What it does

What PDEA does.

Everything listed here ships in version 3.0.1. Nothing is planned or in progress.

Play

  • Add folders to your library, see them in the sidebar and play them
  • Plays MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV and WebM
  • Play, pause, skip forward and back
  • Step forward or back one frame at a time
  • Speed control
  • Play the next video, or preview it
  • Hold the spacebar to play at a different speed while you hold it
  • Loop a video
  • Sound control
  • Cinema mode
  • A sidebar for your library

Ambiance

  • Play songs you already have, on loop
  • Mix and match several at once
  • A volume slider for each one
  • A master volume for all of them

Download

  • Needs an add-on such as yt-dlp installed
  • Direct — give it a link, get the best quality available
  • Simple — pick the quality from a dropdown
  • Advanced — choose the video format, the audio format and the language
  • Playlist — download a whole playlist
  • Pause, resume and cancel while it downloads

Bookmarks

  • Save the web address of any video
  • Keep them in categories

Subscriptions

  • Enter a source link
  • When a new video is out it downloads itself, or waits for you
  • In the quality you prefer

Find

  • Search your videos fast
  • A ranking algorithm puts the best match at the top
  • Beats the search Windows already gives you

Private area

  • Opens only with a passcode
  • Locks itself again when the timer runs out
  • One click to lock it again straight away
  • Nothing in here is visible anywhere else in the app

Built-in browser

  • A browser built into the app
