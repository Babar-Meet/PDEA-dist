What changed

Version 3 was a rebuild of the download queue. Version 3.0.1 moves the download page to a new host. Here is each one against the one before it.

v3.0.1 Latest 13 Oct
  • The download page now lives on Cloudflare Pages instead of Vercel, so it stays up.
  • The update button in the app points at the new address.
Download v3.0.1
v3.0.0 13 Oct
  • Cancel, pause and resume tell the truth. They used to report success even when nothing happened, so a stalled download looked identical to a working one.
  • A row you are acting on cannot be overwritten by a stale progress update while your click is still in flight.
  • Cancelling one download no longer deletes another’s partial files.
  • Deleting is confined to your library folders and only ever touches regular files.
  • Header reduced to two buttons: who made it, and an update prompt that only appears when a newer build exists.
